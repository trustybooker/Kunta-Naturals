# Kunta Naturals

**Pure. Rooted. Real.**

Kunta Naturals is a rooted natural self-care and social-commerce brand system focused on simple body-care, scent, hair/scalp, and calming self-care rituals for real life.

The brand helps people stop buying random self-care products and build one simple natural-inspired ritual that fits their body, scent, hair, and lifestyle.

## What this repo contains

- Public brand system
- Static launch website
- Free lead magnet content
- Paid digital product content
- Affiliate product placeholder catalog
- Short-form video scripts and AI video prompts
- Email welcome sequence
- Compliance guardrails
- Autonomous system architecture
- 14-day launch plan

## First launch path

1. Publish the static site from `/site`.
2. Connect `kuntanaturals.com`.
3. Add real checkout links and affiliate links in `/data/affiliate-products.json`.
4. Launch the free quiz/guide.
5. Sell the $9 starter guide.
6. Add the $27 bundle.
7. Add the $47 vault.
8. Test demand before creating physical inventory.

## Safety rule

Do not publish health cures, disease claims, guaranteed results, fake before/after claims, fake testimonials, or undisclosed affiliate promotions.
