# Kunta Naturals Brand Doctrine

## Locked identity

Name: Kunta Naturals
Tagline: Pure. Rooted. Real.
Logo: rooted K mark with leaf and root symbolism.

The logo is locked as the core identity. Future pages, product labels, social content, and dashboards must use this identity unless the owner approves a formal redesign.

## Universal position

Kunta Naturals has an African sound and rooted feeling, but the public message must stay universal and easy to understand.

Public promise:

Stop buying random self-care products. Build one simple natural ritual that fits your body, scent, hair, and lifestyle.

## Category lock

Kunta Naturals is a ritual-guided natural self-care commerce brand.

It is not just a product store. It is not only beauty. It is not only wellness. It is a guided routine brand.

## Core ritual

Cleanse. Polish. Moisturize. Scent. Reset.

## Belief stack

Belief: self-care should be simple, grounded, and repeatable.
Pain: people are tired of buying random products and still not having a routine.
Remedy: start with one simple ritual.
Solution: quiz, guide, routine, and product path.
Dream: a calm shelf, a fresh rhythm, and a scent that feels personal.
Future: the customer feels more ordered, fresh, confident, and intentional.

## Proof stack

Old proof: ritual, scent, oils, washing, grooming, and plant-based care have existed across cultures for generations.
Current proof: people already buy body care, grooming, scent, bathroom, and self-care products every day.
Current pain: product overload creates decision fatigue and wasted money.
Future result: a clearer ritual makes self-care easier to repeat and easier to buy for.

## Public guardrails

Do not reveal internal strategy on the public site.
Do not use fake results.
Do not use fake testimonials.
Do not imply medical outcomes.
Do not make the brand feel women-only.
Do not make the name feel narrow or hard to understand.
Do not over-explain the brand origin publicly.

## Public voice

Warm. Rooted. Simple. Confident. Universal. Premium.
