# Kunta Naturals Marketing System

## Business model

Kunta Naturals should be a belief-driven ritual brand, not a random product store.

Owned assets:
- Brand
- Domain
- Email list
- Digital products
- Ritual framework
- Product-selection logic
- Content system

Middleman assets:
- Amazon affiliate products
- Partner store products
- Shopify products
- Future physical bundles

Owned future products:
- Kunta Naturals ritual kits
- Digital plus physical bundles
- Branded body-care tools
- Branded scent and reset products

## Niche lock

Ritual-guided natural self-care commerce for people overwhelmed by random product buying.

## Public conversion path

1. Belief: self-care should be simple, rooted, and repeatable.
2. Pain: random products create clutter and confusion.
3. Remedy: one ritual path.
4. Solution: quiz, guide, and product shelf.
5. Dream: a routine that feels personal and calm.
6. Future: confidence, freshness, order, and intention.

## Public homepage flow

Hero promise.
Pain section.
Rituals for real life.
Quiz.
Free guide.
Paid guides.
Product shelf.
Email course.

## Content quality rules

Every content asset should answer one clear question:
- What should I stop doing?
- What simple ritual should I start?
- What product role does this solve?
- What does the future self feel like?
- What should I click next?

## Strongest public hook

Stop buying random self-care products.

## Strongest CTA

Take the free ritual quiz.

## Product bridge

Do not sell product first. Sell the ritual first. Product comes after the ritual is chosen.

## What not to publish

- Internal strategy language
- Sourcing notes
- Weak AI-looking human images
- Medical outcome claims
- Fake proof
- Fake reviews
- Overcomplicated product grids
- Too many choices before the quiz

## Proof approach

Use old proof carefully: ritual, bathing, grooming, oils, scent, and plant-based care have existed across cultures for generations.
Use current proof carefully: people already buy self-care products, but need a simpler way to choose.
Use future proof emotionally: simple daily order creates a better identity.
