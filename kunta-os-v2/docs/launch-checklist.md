# Kunta Naturals OS v2 Launch Checklist

## Must stay locked

- Logo: rooted K leaf mark
- Tagline: Pure. Rooted. Real.
- Niche: ritual-guided natural self-care commerce
- Core ritual: cleanse, polish, moisturize, scent, reset
- Public hook: Stop buying random self-care products.
- Main CTA: Take the free ritual quiz.

## Before public publishing

- Confirm no internal strategy appears on the public site.
- Confirm no fake-looking people images are used.
- Confirm every product has a role in the ritual.
- Confirm affiliate disclosure is visible where needed.
- Confirm every link works.
- Confirm every image is licensed, owned, official, or approved.
- Confirm product prices are current.
- Confirm product descriptions are customer-facing and simple.

## Before Amazon product promotion

- Use official Amazon Special Links.
- Add required Amazon Associate disclosure.
- Do not imply Amazon sponsorship.
- Do not copy restricted product media unless allowed.
- Do not make medical or outcome claims.
- Keep ASIN, link, ritual role, image, and notes tracked in the OS.

## Before custom branded product launch

- Confirm repeated demand from clicks, signups, or sales.
- Confirm supplier cost and margin.
- Confirm packaging path.
- Confirm trademark and brand usage risks.
- Confirm shipping, return, and customer support workflow.

## Secure admin requirements

- Deploy OS v2 to Vercel or similar app host.
- Connect Supabase.
- Run schema SQL.
- Create owner account.
- Protect dashboard routes.
- Connect storage bucket for media.
- Connect analytics.
- Connect email capture.
- Connect product destination links.
