The 3-Minute Natural Self-Care Guide

Promise: Stop buying random self-care products. Build one simple ritual first, then shop only what fits.

The five ritual roles

  1. Cleanse
  2. Polish
  3. Moisturize
  4. Scent
  5. Reset

Minute 1: Cleanse

Choose one cleanser, soap, or body wash that you like using. Keep it simple. The goal is to start fresh without creating confusion.

Minute 2: Polish

Use one gentle tool, such as a luffa, washcloth, or body brush. Use light pressure and keep tools clean and dry between uses.

Minute 3: Moisturize, scent, and reset

Choose one moisture step and one scent or calm cue. End with order, not clutter.
