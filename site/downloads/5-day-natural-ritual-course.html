5-Day Natural Ritual Course

Day 1: Stop buying random products

List what you own, then label every item by role: cleanse, polish, moisturize, scent, or reset.

Day 2: Build the cleanse step

Choose one cleanser you will actually use. Keep the routine simple.

Day 3: Add one gentle tool

Choose a luffa, washcloth, or brush. Use light pressure and keep tools clean and dry.

Day 4: Finish with moisture and scent

Choose one moisture step and one scent cue that fits your mood and budget.

Day 5: Reset the shelf

Remove clutter, keep only current ritual products visible, and plan your next seven days.
