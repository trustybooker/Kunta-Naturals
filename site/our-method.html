The five-role method

Give every product a job—or let it go.

Kunta Naturals is a practical system for organizing self-care choices around five roles: cleanse, polish, moisturize, scent, and reset.

Use the method freeSee the five steps

How it works

Five roles. One routine you can explain.

01

Cleanse

Choose one cleansing step you understand and will actually repeat.

02

Polish

Use one comfortable tool gently and care for it according to its directions.

03

Moisturize

Select one product that fits your preferences, budget, and product label.

04

Scent

Add one optional cue that suits your taste and the people sharing your space.

05

Reset

Put products and tools back so tomorrow’s routine is easier to begin.

Why this exists

Self-care should reduce decisions, not create more of them.

The method was created to help people name what each product does, use what they already own, recognize duplicates, and wait before buying another trend-driven item.

The buying filter

  1. What role does it fill?
  2. Will I use it this week?
  3. Does it replace something?
  4. Does the claim sound supportable?
  5. Does it fit my planned budget?

Editorial standard

Useful, transparent, and inside our scope.

What Kunta Naturals does

We provide routine frameworks, worksheets, organization tools, and buying filters for general self-care decisions.

What Kunta Naturals does not do

We do not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment, promise physical results, or replace advice from qualified professionals.

How recommendations are made

Recommendations are based on the visitor’s stated routine goal and the smallest Kunta Naturals resource that fits it. Paid products are not recommended when a free starting point is enough.
