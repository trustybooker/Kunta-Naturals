# Kunta Naturals Digital Products Quality Audit

## Verdict

The original digital products were directionally correct but too light for a serious paid offer. They needed stronger structure, clearer buyer outcomes, safer language, and more owned assets that Kunta Naturals can sell without relying on inventory or shipping.

## Quality standard

Every Kunta Naturals digital product must be:

- Owned by Kunta Naturals
- Easy to understand
- Printable or usable on a phone
- Safe from medical or exaggerated claims
- Connected to the core ritual: cleanse, polish, moisturize, scent, reset
- Useful before the customer buys physical products
- Strong enough to sell without feeling like filler

## What belongs in physical products

Physical products should support the digital ritual path. They should not replace it.

Allowed models:

- Amazon or partner referral links
- Shopify Collective or partner fulfilled products
- Print-on-demand branded products
- Private-label supplier fulfillment after demand is proven

Kunta Naturals should not store, pack, ship, or deliver physical products.

## What must not be sold as digital content

- Generic AI affirmations with no routine value
- Fake scientific claims
- Fake testimonials
- Copy-paste wellness advice
- Overcomplicated planners nobody will use
- Children-focused product claims
- Medical, cure, treatment, or guaranteed-result promises

## Final product rule

The digital products sell the method. The physical products support the method.
