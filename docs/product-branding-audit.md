# Kunta Naturals Product Branding Audit

## Issue found

Some product cards and digital covers did not make the Kunta Naturals logo visible enough.

## Fix applied

- Added a visible Kunta Naturals brand strip to every dynamic product card.
- Enlarged the logo badge on product media.
- Added a brand fallback for static product cards.
- Updated the guide cover with the official Kunta Naturals identity.
- Regenerated the private digital product PDF exports with the Kunta Naturals logo on every cover and page header/footer.

## Brand rule going forward

Every Kunta Naturals product should show:

1. Official logo or logo mark.
2. Kunta Naturals brand name.
3. Pure. Rooted. Real.
4. Product title.
5. Ritual role or clear product purpose.

## Verdict

Safe to merge. This patch improves visible branding and does not add private files to the public site.
