# Natural Body-Care Starter Checklist

## Purpose

Use this checklist before buying more products. The goal is to build a routine that is simple enough to repeat and clear enough to shop for.

## The Kunta Naturals buying rule

Do not buy by hype. Buy by ritual role.

Every product should answer one question:

What job does this do in my routine?

## Core ritual roles

### 1. Cleanse

- [ ] One soap, cleanser, or body wash
- [ ] I like the scent or feel
- [ ] I understand when I will use it
- [ ] It does not make exaggerated claims

### 2. Polish

- [ ] One luffa, washcloth, or body brush
- [ ] I can use it gently
- [ ] I can clean and dry it properly
- [ ] I know when it should be replaced

### 3. Moisturize

- [ ] One body oil, lotion, or body butter
- [ ] I like the texture
- [ ] I would use it after showering
- [ ] I do not need three versions yet

### 4. Scent

- [ ] One scent cue
- [ ] It does not overpower the routine
- [ ] It fits my mood: fresh, warm, clean, soft, earthy, or calm
- [ ] I understand how it fits with my moisture step

### 5. Reset

- [ ] One towel, robe, basket, tray, checklist card, or planner
- [ ] It makes the bathroom or self-care space easier to use
- [ ] It helps me repeat the routine

## Family bathroom reset items

Use these for organization and routine flow, not health claims.

- [ ] Clean towel area
- [ ] Bath-time checklist card
- [ ] Small product basket
- [ ] Hair-care tool area
- [ ] Dirty towel / laundry spot
- [ ] Simple reset card on the bathroom shelf

## Men’s grooming reset items

- [ ] One body wash or soap
- [ ] One grooming tool
- [ ] One moisture step
- [ ] One scent cue
- [ ] One towel or robe reset item

## Skip for now

- [ ] Duplicate products
- [ ] Random viral products
- [ ] Harsh tools you do not know how to use
- [ ] Products with claims you do not understand
- [ ] Expensive items that do not fit your ritual role
- [ ] Anything you only want because the video looked good

## Final checklist before buying

Before purchasing, answer:

1. What ritual role does this product fill?
2. Will I use it this week?
3. Does it replace something or add clutter?
4. Is there a simpler version?
5. Is the claim believable?
6. Does it fit my budget?

If you cannot answer these questions, wait before buying.
