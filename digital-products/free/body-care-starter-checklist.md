# Natural Body-Care Starter Checklist

Use this checklist before buying more products.

## Core starter items

- [ ] Luffa or body brush
- [ ] Gentle soap or cleanser
- [ ] Body oil
- [ ] Body butter or lotion
- [ ] Soft towel
- [ ] Simple scent product
- [ ] Candle or calm reset item
- [ ] Scalp brush
- [ ] Silk or satin night accessory
- [ ] Small basket or shelf area for ritual products

## Buy first

Pick only one item from each category:

1. Cleanse
2. Exfoliate
3. Moisturize
4. Scent
5. Reset

## Skip for now

- Duplicates
- Random viral products
- Harsh scrubs
- Products with claims you do not understand
- Items you only want because the video looked good
