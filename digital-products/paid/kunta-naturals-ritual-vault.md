## Hair & Scalp Ritual Guide

### Goal

Create a simple wash-day support routine without making unsupported hair growth claims.

### Steps

1. Prepare the space
2. Detangle or prep as needed
3. Use scalp brush gently
4. Cleanse
5. Moisturize or oil according to preference
6. Protect at night

### Scalp brush safety note

Use gentle pressure. Stop if the tool feels uncomfortable.

## Calm Night Reset Guide

### Goal

End the day with a simple calming ritual.

### Steps

1. Warm shower or face/body reset
2. Light body moisture
3. Soft scent cue
4. Candle or low-light cue
5. Clean towel or robe
6. Put products back in place

## Routine friction audit

Mark every source of friction:

- I cannot find what I need.
- Too many products compete for the same role.
- The routine takes longer than the time I have.
- I do not enjoy the scent or texture.
- I forget the final reset.
- The products exceed my planned budget.
- I keep buying before finishing.

Choose one friction point to remove this week. Do not solve all of them by buying something.

## 30-day simplification review

At the end of 30 days:

1. Which products did I actually use?
2. Which role was duplicated?

5. What needs replacement—not expansion?

## Vault value test

The Vault has done its job when the customer has:

- one written core ritual;

- a weekly maintenance rhythm;
- a clear skip list;

## Scope and safety

The Vault is for self-care organization, preferences, and buying decisions. It does not diagnose skin, hair, scalp, sleep, or health conditions. Follow product labels, stop using products that cause discomfort, and seek qualified professional advice when appropriate.
