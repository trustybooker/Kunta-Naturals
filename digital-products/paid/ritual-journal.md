# Kunta Naturals Ritual Journal

## Product type

Owned digital product. Can later become print-on-demand.

## Product promise

A guided journal for turning self-care from random products into a simple repeatable ritual.

This journal is not medical advice. It is a daily reflection and routine-building tool.

## How to use this journal

Use one page per day. Keep the answers short. The goal is not perfection. The goal is awareness, consistency, and cleaner choices.
