# The Kunta Naturals 7-Day Body Ritual Guide

## Product promise

Build a simple natural-inspired body-care ritual in one week.

This is not a miracle plan. It is a practical routine builder. By the end, the customer should know what to use, when to use it, what to stop buying, and how to make the ritual feel easy.

## How to use this guide

Each day has one focus:

1. Choose your ritual direction
2. Simplify your shower
3. Add exfoliation
4. Add moisture
5. Add scent
6. Build your space
7. Lock the routine

## Day 1: Choose your ritual direction

Pick one:

- Glow: polished, moisturized-feeling routine
- Fresh: simple clean shower routine
- Scent: signature scent and body-care pairing
- Scalp: hair/scalp wash-day support
- Calm: night reset and peaceful routine

Do not choose all five. Start with one.

3. Moisturize after

## Day 3: Add exfoliation with intention

Choose either a luffa or body brush. The goal is not harsh scrubbing. Use light pressure and keep the routine comfortable.

Checklist:
- [ ] Tool is clean and dry between uses
- [ ] I use light pressure
- [ ] I do not overdo the routine
- [ ] I replace tools when needed

## Day 4: Add moisture

After showering, use body oil, body butter, or lotion. Apply while your body still feels slightly damp.

- Believing guaranteed-result claims

- Ignoring comfort and consistency
