## Body ritual framework

A complete ritual has five steps:

1. Cleanse
2. Polish
3. Moisturize
4. Layer scent
5. Reset the space

## Moisture layering guide

Layer 1: Damp body after shower  
Layer 2: Body oil or body butter  
Layer 3: Light scent cue  
Layer 4: Soft clothing/towel  

Do not add too many strong scents at once.

## Beginner scent layering guide

Choose one scent family:

- Fresh: clean, bright, light
- Warm: vanilla, amber, soft spice
- Earthy: wood, musk, herbal
- Sweet: soft gourmand
- Calm: lavender-style, soft powder, cozy

Simple formula:
Clean base + moisture + scent cue + space cue

## Shopping decision tree

Before buying, ask:

1. Does this product fit my ritual type?
2. Does it replace something or add clutter?
3. Can I explain when I will use it?
4. Does the seller make unsupported claims?
5. Is this a need, a test, or a random impulse?

## The scent-conflict audit

List the scented items already in the routine: cleanser, lotion or oil, fragrance, deodorant, hair product, laundry product, and room scent.

For each item ask:

1. Is this meant to be noticed or stay in the background?
2. Does it support the primary direction?
3. Can I use an unscented version to reduce competition?
4. Am I layering because I enjoy it or because a trend told me to?

## Three layering levels

### Quiet

Unscented or lightly scented cleanse + moisture + one scent cue.

### Balanced

Compatible cleanse + neutral moisture + one personal scent + optional room cue.

### Expressive

Coordinated cleanse + moisture + personal scent. Add intensity slowly and stay considerate of shared spaces.

## Comfort and consideration

- Follow product labels and avoid unsupported claims.
- Stop using a topical product that causes discomfort and seek qualified advice when needed.
- Fragrance preferences and sensitivities vary; use less in shared spaces.
- Never mix products in ways their makers do not direct.
- This is a planning and preference tool, not skin or health advice.
