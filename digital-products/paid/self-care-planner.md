# Kunta Naturals Self-Care Planner

## Product type

Owned digital product. Can later become a print-on-demand planner.

## Product promise

Plan your ritual, choose products with intention, and stop building a shelf full of things you do not use.

This planner is a routine and organization tool. It does not give medical advice or promise physical results.

## Section 4: Shopping filter

Before buying, answer:

1. What role does this product fill?
2. Do I already own something similar?
3. Will I use it this week?
4. Is the price aligned with my budget?
5. Is the claim simple and believable?
6. Does this make the ritual easier?

Decision:

- [ ] Buy now
- [ ] Save for later
- [ ] Skip
