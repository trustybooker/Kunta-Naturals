# Kunta Naturals OS

## Purpose

Kunta Naturals OS is the autonomous operating system for the brand. It researches trends, drafts content, scores product ideas, prepares affiliate placements, writes digital products, checks compliance, and recommends weekly next moves.

## Human approval required

The system must ask for owner approval before:

- Publishing content
- Sending mass emails
- Spending ad money
- Changing prices
- Adding official affiliate links
- Making product claims
- Launching a physical product
- Posting realistic AI-generated people or testimonials

## Modules

### 1. Brand Brain

Stores brand voice, taglines, offer ladder, forbidden claims, visual direction, approved CTAs, and product category rules.

### 2. Trend Scout

Tracks body-care routines, luffa content, scent layering, shower reset videos, scalp brush content, calm night rituals, natural-inspired routines, and social commerce product angles.

### 3. Product Curator

Scores each idea from 1-10 on demand, profit potential, brand fit, content potential, compliance risk, fulfillment ease, and trust risk.

### 4. Offer Builder

Creates lead magnets, paid digital products, product descriptions, upsells, email sequences, landing pages, and affiliate collection pages.

### 5. Video Factory

Creates hook, voiceover, caption, scene direction, AI video prompt, CTA, and funnel destination.

### 6. Compliance Guard

Blocks cure claims, disease claims, guaranteed claims, fake testimonials, fake before/after claims, undisclosed affiliate promotions, and unsupported organic or clinical claims.

### 7. Publishing Queue

Each post has status, platform, caption, hashtags, CTA, disclosure, destination URL, and compliance score.

### 8. Analytics Optimizer

Weekly report includes top hooks, weak hooks, best products, best CTAs, best funnel destination, and next product recommendation.
