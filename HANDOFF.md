# Kunta Naturals Build Handoff

## Goal

Turn this repository into a live launch site and content system.

## Included

- Static website in /site
- Brand system in /brand
- Digital products in /digital-products
- Affiliate placeholders in /data
- Video scripts and prompts in /content
- Automation plan in /automation
- Launch docs in /docs

## Next steps

1. Deploy the /site folder.
2. Connect kuntanaturals.com.
3. Add the email capture link.
4. Add checkout links for the digital products.
5. Add official affiliate links in /data/affiliate-products.json.
6. Format the digital products as PDFs or protected digital downloads.
7. Add analytics and UTM tracking.
8. Keep human approval before posting, pricing changes, affiliate link changes, or ad spend.

## Guardrails

- No fake testimonials.
- No medical or cure claims.
- No ad spend without approval.
- No unsupported organic, clinical, or FDA claims.
- No undisclosed affiliate promotions.
