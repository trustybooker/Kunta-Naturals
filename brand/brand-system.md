# Kunta Naturals Brand System

## Public position

Kunta Naturals is a natural-inspired self-care brand built around simple daily rituals: cleanse, exfoliate, moisturize, layer scent, and reset.

## Core promise

Simple natural rituals for real life.

## Main tagline

Pure. Rooted. Real.

## Secondary taglines

- Natural Rituals for Real Life.
- Rooted Care. Natural Confidence.
- Less Products. Better Ritual.
- Your 3-Minute Natural Reset.

## Customer avatar

Busy adults who want natural-feeling self-care without complicated routines. They are tired of buying random products from social media. They want a simple guide that tells them what to use first, how to use it, and what to skip.

## Brand voice

Warm, calm, simple, honest, practical, grounded, confident.

## Words to use

rooted, simple, ritual, natural-inspired, clean, fresh, calm, glow, scent, reset, routine, beginner-friendly, real life, less clutter, body care, self-care.

## Words to avoid

cure, treat, heal disease, guaranteed, miracle, instant transformation, doctor-approved unless verified, FDA-approved unless verified, organic unless certified, clinically proven unless supported, secret ancient cure, fake testimonial language.

## Visual direction

- Cream background
- Deep brown text
- Clay and warm gold accents
- Leaf green for nature cues
- Natural light
- Bathroom counter scenes
- Hands-only demos
- Water, luffa texture, soap foam, towels, oils, candles, clean shelves

## Brand catch

Stop buying random self-care products. Build one simple ritual that fits your body, scent, hair, and lifestyle.

## Public brand story

Kunta Naturals was created for people who want self-care to feel simple, real, and grounded again. The brand focuses on natural-inspired body-care routines, practical guides, and beginner-friendly product recommendations that help customers feel clean, fresh, confident, and rooted in their daily ritual.
