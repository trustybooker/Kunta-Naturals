# Kunta Naturals Claims Guard

Use this checklist before publishing any page, video, caption, email, product description, or ad.

## Hard blocks

Do not publish:

- Cure claims
- Disease claims
- Guaranteed result claims
- Fake before/after claims
- Fake testimonials
- Unsupported medical or health claims
- Unsupported organic claims
- Unsupported Made in USA claims
- FDA approved claims for cosmetics or general body-care products
- Clinically proven claims unless there is credible documentation
- Any affiliate recommendation without a clear disclosure
- Any AI-generated realistic person presented as a real customer

## Safe language

Use:

- supports a simple routine
- helps you build a ritual
- natural-inspired
- beginner-friendly
- designed for daily self-care
- designed to feel simple and grounded
- helps reduce routine confusion
- use as part of your body-care routine

## Risky language rewrites

Instead of: heals dry skin  
Use: supports a moisturized-feeling routine

Instead of: cures acne  
Use: build a simple cleansing routine

Instead of: guaranteed glow  
Use: a simple routine for a fresh, polished look

Instead of: ancient secret  
Use: rooted in simple natural-care habits

## Affiliate disclosure

Use this wording near affiliate links:

Some product links may be affiliate or referral links. Kunta Naturals may earn a commission if you purchase through them, at no extra cost to you.

## AI video disclosure

If a platform requires disclosure for realistic AI-generated or meaningfully altered video, label it clearly using the platform's disclosure tools and/or caption language.
